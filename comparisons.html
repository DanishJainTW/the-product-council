How they were built

From the curated competitor packs, the processed research notes and a static reading of each competitor's Android apps and web portal code, October 2026. Code reading shows a feature exists in the build, not that every user reaches it, so those answers carry an "in build" tag. Unknowns are shown as unknown.

Update a comparison when its competitor pack changes. The pages live in docs/comparisons/.
