What STA's payment portal shows

Student Transportation of America runs a separate website for payments. Only its sign-in page is public. The strip below is that portal. Every square is one thing we looked for, grouped by area and coloured by what we found.

Built from our reading of the public sign-in page, October 2026. We did not sign in or fill in any form. We have no other payment portal in our research yet, so this page describes one portal and compares nothing.

The portal in plain words

What the portal seems to be for, what stands out, and where it falls short.

What we could not see

  • We saw only the public sign-in page. Everything after sign-in is hidden, and the site sends no app code we could read.
  • We did not sign in, create an account or submit any form.
  • What the portal charges for, and who pays, is our inference from the page title and the card payment library. It most likely serves parents or guardians. That is not confirmed.
  • Payment screens, amounts owed, saved cards, receipts and refunds are handled on STA's server. None of them were visible.
  • The password reset page was linked but not opened.
  • Staff or admin roles, emails and other notifications were not visible.
  • This is the only payment portal in our research, so there is nothing to set beside it yet.
