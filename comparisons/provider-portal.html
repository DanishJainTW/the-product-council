How three competitors work with transport providers

A provider portal is where a transport company picks up trips, assigns its drivers, proves they are compliant and gets paid. The three competitors run this in very different ways. Each strip below is one company. Every square is one job a provider can do.

EverDriven works through local transport companies, as FirstAlt does. HopSkipDrive uses gig drivers and brings in partner fleets only for wheelchair rides. Zum owns its fleet and employs its drivers, so it has no provider portal. Built from our competitor research packs and our reading of the portal code, October 2026.

What we could not see

  • We read the EverDriven portal's sign-in page and main code only. About 20 screens load after sign-in, so trip offers, at-risk trips, compliance and pay are seen in part.
  • EverDriven pays providers every Friday, yet the portal also shows daily pay summaries. How the two fit is open.
  • HopSkipDrive's partner portal sits behind a login. Only its help articles are public, and they cover accepting requests and assigning drivers.
  • Zum's school portal code has records for vendors and vendor staff behind a switch. Who uses them is unknown.
  • Beacon's ADROIT also places drivers through transport companies, like FirstAlt. It is not part of this comparison.
